Looking After Me
  • My human will add you to my Tractive account - use tractive to keep tabs on my activity or to find me if I should get lost.
  • Make sure your wifi is added to my Tractive Safe Places - this stops the batteries running down to quickly.
  • I take a tablet every day - it's for my wobbly elbow and I love it when it's wrapped in a bit of cheese or ham. I take this at around 17:45 - but don't give me it if I'm having tummy troubles.
  • I am on one scoop of dry food a day (that's roughly 180 g) - feed it to me in the evenings around 18:45. If I don't eat it straight away don't worry, I will come back to it later.
  • On my food i get a sprinkle of natural joint supplements (about half a teaspoon) - just sprinkle it on top of my bisuits.
  • There's no hard maximum on how many treats I am allowed - but I do have a sensitive tummy so don't go overboard: you will pay the consequences.
  • Speaking of consequences... if I need an emergency wee or poo I will try and tell you: I will come up to you, I won't settle, I may run back and forth to the door if I've worked out where it is and I will get incredibly excited when you start to make "going outside" actions.
  • I love to play with my toys - I have a few favourites but I'm always happy to try new ones.
  • I love to go for walks and I normally go out between 2 and 4 times a day. I'm not a fan of the rain so I might prefer going into the yard for 5 minutes on a very wet day. I'll usually let you know by getting straight in my bed after you put me in my harness.
